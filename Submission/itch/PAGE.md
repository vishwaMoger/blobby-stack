## Description

Blobby Stack is a cute one-tap stacking game. Blocks slide in from the side: tap at just the right moment to
hop over them and land on top. Nail the timing for a PERFECT landing and chain bonus points, but jump too early
or too late and you'll get bumped off the tower!

Climb through 7 colorful worlds, each with its own blocks: moon rocks in the Cosmos, vine-wrapped stalks on the
Beanstalk, coffins on Haunted Hill, hay bales on the Sunset Farm, ovens in the Hot Kitchen, bricks at the Golden
Castle and glowing chips in Neon City.

**Features**

- One-button controls that anyone can learn in seconds
- Perfect-timing streaks worth up to 3 points per block
- 7 worlds to climb through, with new scenery every 20 blocks
- 7 adorable characters to unlock with coins
- Daily rewards and a new Daily Tower challenge every day
- Local 2 Player race: share one keyboard or one screen with a friend
- No ads

**Controls**

| | Solo | 2 Player |
|---|---|---|
| Desktop | Space, W, Up arrow or left click to jump | Player 1: W, Player 2: Up arrow |
| Mobile | Tap anywhere to jump | Tap your half of the screen |
| Pause | Esc or P | |

Progress (coins, best score, unlocks) is saved in your browser.
